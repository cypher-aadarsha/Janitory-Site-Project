Sustainable Operations Framework

Our daily cleaning protocols are engineered around three core environmental pillars.

Safe Chemicals

We exclusively deploy EPA Safer Choice and Green Seal™ certified concentrated solutions. These high-performance cleaners are completely free of corrosive compounds, heavy metals, and toxic VOCs.

Waste Diversion

Our comprehensive recycling management and strict composting programs assist large commercial properties and LEED-certified complexes in achieving aggressive zero-waste sustainability targets.

Energy Conservation

From utilizing specialized High-Efficiency Particulate Air (HEPA) backpack vacuums to implementing strategic "day-cleaning" schedules that significantly reduce your facility's evening lighting overhead.
