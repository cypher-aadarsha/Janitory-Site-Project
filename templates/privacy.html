Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 1, 2026  •  Last Updated: September 1, 2026

{{ site_settings.site_name|default:"24 Hours Facility Maintenance Inc." }} ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information of visitors to our website at 24hoursfacilitymaintenance.com (the "Site") and of individuals who contact us about our commercial cleaning, janitorial, and facility maintenance services.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices available to you. By using our Site or submitting information to us through a quote request, contact form, phone call, or client portal, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Introduction

This policy covers information collected through our Site, quote requests, our contact form, phone calls to the number listed on our Site, and our client portal.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide to Us

When you request a quote, contact us, or use our client portal, we may collect:

  • Name and job title
  • Company, property management firm, or property name
  • Business address and property location
  • Email address and phone number
  • Details about the service you are requesting (e.g., facility type, square footage, service frequency)
  • Any other information you choose to include in a message to us

2.2 Information We Collect Automatically

When you visit our Site, we and our service providers automatically collect certain information through cookies, tags, and similar technologies, including:

  • IP address and approximate location
  • Browser type, device type, and operating system
  • Pages viewed, time on page, and referring or exit pages
  • Interactions with our forms and click-to-call links
  • Identifiers used for advertising and analytics measurement (e.g., Google click identifiers)

2.3 Information from Phone Calls

If you call the phone number displayed on our Site, that call and any information you provide during it (such as your name, phone number, and service needs) may be recorded or logged for quality assurance, training, and lead-tracking purposes, where permitted by law and disclosed to you at the start of the call.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies, deployed through Google Tag Manager, including:

  • Google Analytics (GA4) — to understand how visitors use our Site, including pages visited, session duration, and conversion events such as form submissions and phone clicks.
  • Google Ads Conversion Tracking and Remarketing — to measure the effectiveness of our advertising and to show relevant ads to past visitors of our Site as they browse other websites.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking or deleting cookies may affect Site functionality. Where required by law, we request your consent before placing non-essential cookies and honor your choice through our cookie banner.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the personal information we collect to:

  • Respond to quote requests and inquiries
  • Schedule and provide facility maintenance and cleaning services
  • Communicate with you about your account, service, or request
  • Improve our Site, services, and marketing effectiveness
  • Measure and optimize our advertising campaigns
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements
  • Protect against fraud, unauthorized transactions, and security incidents

We do not use your information to make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects concerning you.

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We may share personal information with:

  • Service Providers — companies that help us operate our business, such as website hosting, scheduling, and marketing analytics providers, under contracts that limit their use of your information to providing services to us.
  • Advertising Partners — Google, for advertising and remarketing as described in Section 3. Sharing information with Google for cross-context behavioral advertising (such as remarketing) may be considered “sharing” under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA). See Section 7 for your opt-out rights.
  • Professional Advisors — attorneys, accountants, and consultants, as needed.
  • Legal and Safety — to comply with a legal obligation, respond to lawful requests by public authorities, or protect the rights, property, or safety of the Company, our clients, or others.
  • Business Transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of business assets.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including to provide services, maintain business records, and comply with legal, accounting, or reporting obligations. Retention periods vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it was collected.

7. Your California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, the CCPA, as amended by the CPRA, provides you with the following rights regarding your personal information, subject to certain exceptions:

  • Right to Know — request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you, and the categories of sources, purposes, and third parties with whom it was shared.
  • Right to Delete — request deletion of personal information we have collected from you, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to Correct — request correction of inaccurate personal information we maintain about you.
  • Right to Opt Out of Sale/Sharing — we do not sell personal information for money. To the extent our use of advertising cookies (such as Google Ads remarketing) is considered “sharing” under the CPRA, you may opt out using the cookie preferences on our Site or by contacting us.
  • Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information — we do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information (as defined by the CPRA) through our Site beyond what is necessary to provide our services.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination — we will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the information in Section 11. We may need to verify your identity before completing your request. You may also designate an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf.

8. Data Security

We maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

Our Site and services are intended for business and commercial use and are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

10. Third-Party Links

Our Site may contain links to third-party websites, including social media platforms and our client portal provider. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party site you visit.
