About Us

Welcome to 24 Hours Facility Maintenance Inc., your trusted partner in comprehensive, around-the-clock facility care. With over 15 years of industry experience and a relentless commitment to excellence, we have built our reputation on reliability, professionalism, and the highest standards of service.

Our Story

Founded in 2011 under a different name, we recognized the need for continuous, dependable building maintenance and launched 24 Hours Facility Maintenance Inc. in 2023. Since our inception, we have partnered with more than 300 clients—including global brands like FedEx, Best Western, Holiday Inn Express, Hyatt, and America’s Best Value Inn—delivering solutions that keep businesses running smoothly and safely, day and night.

The COVID-19 pandemic put our resilience and commitment to the test, and we rose to the challenge by maintaining the highest standards of sanitation and safety for our clients, staff, and the communities we serve. Our adaptability and dedication have become the foundation of our ongoing success.

Our Mission

"Our mission is to provide our clients and employees with a clean, safe, and healthy environment through responsive, world-class maintenance services. As professionals serving professionals, we approach every job with integrity, transparency, and an unwavering focus on quality, ensuring that every facility is a space where businesses can thrive."
