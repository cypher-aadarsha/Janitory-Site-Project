## Project overview

Django site for 24 Hours Facility Maintenance Inc. (commercial cleaning,
Oakland CA). Public marketing pages plus a custom CMS dashboard at
`/dashboard/` for managing services, bookings, inquiries and applications.

- `core/` — models, public views, CMS views, forms
- `janitorial_site/` — settings, URLs, custom middleware
- `templates/` — public templates extend `base.html`; CMS templates extend
  `cms/base_cms.html`

Run tests with `python manage.py test core`.


## Tracking and lead capture

The lead path is business-critical — the site runs paid Google Ads, and a
silent break here costs real money. It has broken before: a field-name
mismatch once made every form submission fail validation while the page
still looked normal.

- The Google tag (GA4 + Google Ads) renders once in `base.html` from the
  `google_tracking` context processor. Never paste tracking snippets into
  individual templates.
- The Ads conversion fires only on the success page after a validated,
  saved booking — never on page load generally, and never on submit.
- Tracking IDs and conversion labels live in settings/env, never inline.
- `AttributionMiddleware` captures gclid/UTM on landing and stores it on
  the booking, so leads can be tied back to ad spend.
- Lead notification emails are best-effort: the booking is saved before
  the email is attempted, and a mail failure must never fail the request.
- The CSP in `janitorial_site/middleware.py` must keep allowing the Google
  Ads hosts (`googleadservices.com`, `googleads.g.doubleclick.net`,
  `td.doubleclick.net`), or conversions are blocked in the browser.

Keep `core/tests.py` passing — it covers tag rendering, the CSP
allowances, form submission, notifications and attribution.
